Borough Variation

  • Manhattan leans toward entire homes, reflecting the high-demand tourist market and higher price points.
  • Brooklyn has a stronger presence of private rooms, consistent with its residential brownstone character.
  • Queens and Bronx have higher proportions of private rooms, driven by lower price points and residential hosting.
  • Staten Island has a notably higher share of entire-home listings relative to its small market size.

Guest Engagement and Review Patterns

Review Activity

  • Steady growth in review activity through 2019
  • Peak months: June and July (summer tourism)
  • Average listing has ~23 reviews
  • About 20% of listings have never been reviewed

Correlations

  • Higher-priced listings tend to receive fewer reviews
  • Reviews per month and total reviews correlate strongly
  • Price and availability show weak negative correlation
  • Minimum nights and reviews are inversely related

Minimum Night Requirements

  • Most listings: 1-3 night minimum
  • ~4-5% require 30+ nights
  • Manhattan has the highest median minimum nights
Regulatory Flag: Listings with 30+ night minimums may represent an attempt to circumvent NYC short-term rental restrictions, or they may be legitimate long-term rentals using the platform.

Recommendations

For Hosts

  • Target $50-200/night price range
  • Brooklyn offers the best demand-to-competition ratio
  • Set minimum nights to 1-2 days
  • Prioritise generating reviews
  • Use appealing, descriptive listing names
  • Maximise calendar availability

For Policy Makers

  • Monitor professional host concentration
  • Investigate 30+ night minimum listings
  • Assess housing impact in dense areas
  • Consider commercial operator registration requirements

For the Platform

  • Promote outer boroughs for market diversification
  • Build tools to support individual hosts
  • Leverage strong review growth as a health signal
